About

About Us

BIG FOOT SPA is a large salon near Ryogoku with about 13 rooms. We offer foot reflexology, aroma oil massage, Bali-style cream head spa, facial care, ear esthe, and fish therapy — plus pair rooms when you want to visit together.

Tourists and locals are welcome; staff can assist in multiple languages. Come after sightseeing around the Sumida area, or unwind after work — a calm space for feet, body, and scalp reset.

Open late

Weekdays until 23:30; weekends & holidays from 10:00 — easy after dinner or sightseeing.
