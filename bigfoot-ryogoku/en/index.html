About

About Us

BIG FOOT SPA is one of Tokyo's few large-scale, full-service massage spas, located in Ryogoku. We offer Japanese・Taiwanese-style body massage, foot reflexology, aroma oil massage, Doctor Fish, Balinese head spa and facial treatments in a calm, comfortable space with private treatment rooms and rooms for couples and groups. Just landed and shaking off flight fatigue? Worn out after a day of sightseeing? Want one last treat before heading home? BIG FOOT SPA is the perfect place to unwind — on your own, or together with friends and family.

Large Salon for Groups, Families & Solo Visits

9 foot chairs, 2 pair rooms, and 10 single-person rooms — comfortable for solo visits or groups of up to 23.

Easy Access, Near the Sights

Just 30 seconds on foot from JR Ryogoku Station's West Exit, surrounded by Ryogoku's business hotels. Asakusa, Senso-ji Temple, Tokyo Skytree, Kinshicho and Asakusabashi are all within easy reach.

Easy for International Guests

Rated with perfect scores on Google and Tripadvisor. Support is available in Japanese, English and Chinese, with menus in Japanese, English, Traditional Chinese and Korean.

Menu & Pricing

All prices include tax. Every foot massage treatment comes with a complimentary foot bath, and foot reflexology sessions of 40 minutes or longer also cover the calves. Aroma oil massage is performed by female therapists — male guests can request a male therapist, based on availability.
