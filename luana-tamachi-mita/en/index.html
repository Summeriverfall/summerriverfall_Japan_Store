About Us

Luana focuses on results: made-to-order fascial release, Eastern-style lymph massage, and Korean korugi small-face care, with therapists who have over ten years of experience.

Guests of all genders are welcome. After hearing your concerns we tailor the session—fair pricing and practical relief, with staff continually refining skill to support local health and beauty.
