About

About Us

MIROKU Asakusa Azumabashi sits between Tokyo Skytree, Asakusa and Oshiage. Exit A3 of Toei Asakusa Line Honjo-azumabashi Station is right in front of the salon — about a 10-second walk.

We offer seitai, foot reflexology, aroma oil and head spa, a good reset after a day of sightseeing. Couples are welcome, and solo visits are just as easy.

Open year-round until 23:00, so you can drop in after work or after exploring the neighborhood.
