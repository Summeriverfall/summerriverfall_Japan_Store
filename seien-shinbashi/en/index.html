About the Salon

星苑 Hoshi no sono is a relax & esthe salon just by Shinbashi Station, known for aroma lymph massage and sleep head spa. Fully private rooms, firmer pressure available, and open 24 hours for after-work or late-night visits.

Choose from head spa, full-body lymph, meridians bodywork, foot care and facial options. English and Chinese support is available, making the salon convenient for travelers, business travelers, and local regulars alike.
