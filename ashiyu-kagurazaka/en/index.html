About us

At Ashiyu Kagurazaka, we specialize in Chinese-style massage and our signature ginger therapy. Using firm and attentive techniques, we carefully relax the body from the feet up to the shoulders and back.

Ginger is known for its detoxifying properties and helps support your health and beauty from within. After a full-body massage to relax stiff muscles, a ginger treatment pack and warm towels help keep the body warm and moisturized. As the ginger’s active ingredients penetrate deep into the body, you can feel your fatigue and swelling noticeably relieved.

We are open until 11:00 PM, so feel free to stop by and unwind after work.
