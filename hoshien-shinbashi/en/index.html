About the Salon

Hoshien sits just by Shinbashi Station, where experienced therapists ease tension with skilled hands — head spa, lymph massage, bodywork, foot reflexology and focused care for shoulders, neck and lower back. Open 24 hours a day, with fully private treatment rooms.

The calm, clean salon is easy to visit between meetings or after work. Pressure can be adjusted from gentle to firm. Choose combinations for eye fatigue, stiff shoulders, lower-back discomfort or swollen legs.
